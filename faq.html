Strip club FAQ: Dream Girls Minneapolis

Straight answers about cover, age, private dances, hours and parties at downtown Minneapolis' full-nude strip club.

Is Dream Girls a strip club or a gentlemen's club?

Both terms describe us. Dream Girls is an 18+ full-nude strip club and gentlemen's club in downtown Minneapolis — nude dancers on stage nightly, private dances on the floor, and a VIP room. We're also a juice bar, which is what makes 18+ entry possible.

Why is Dream Girls 18+ instead of 21+?

Because we serve no alcohol. As a juice bar, Dream Girls admits guests 18 and older with a valid ID. Our 21+ sister club Sneaky Pete's next door has a full bar and food.

How much is cover at Dream Girls?

Cover is $10 until midnight and $15 after midnight. Every guest also orders one beverage — $14 after midnight — so plan on about $29 to walk in with a drink. See the full list of cover deals and free-admission nights on our specials page.

How can I get in free?

Several ways: bring a same-day concert or sporting event ticket stub, celebrate your birthday week with ID, come with a bachelor or bachelorette group of six or more, show a college ID on Thursdays, or a military ID on Wednesdays. Guests coming over from Sneaky Pete's also get free cover.

How much are private dances?

Topless and nude lap dances start at $20 on the floor. Private dance rooms and the VIP room are priced at the club — ask your entertainer or the floor host.

What are Dream Girls' hours?

Wednesday and Thursday 8PM–3AM, Friday and Saturday 8PM–4AM. Closed Monday and Tuesday. Sundays we're open during football season. Open till 4AM on weekends.

Where is Dream Girls located?

12 N 5th St, Minneapolis, MN 55403 — in the heart of downtown Minneapolis. Directions, parking notes and a map are on our contact page.

Can I book a bachelor or bachelorette party?

Yes. Groups of six or more get free cover, and we can have the room ready for birthdays and group nights. Send us the date and headcount through the party booking form on our contact page.

Is the popcorn really free?

Yes — fresh, hot popcorn is free every night we're open. Grab a box, catch the show, come back for refills.

How do I audition to dance at Dream Girls?

Auditions for new exotic dancers are open. You must be 18 or older. Request an audition through the showgirl audition form on our contact page.
