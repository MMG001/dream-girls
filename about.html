Who we are

Downtown's after-dark room

Dream Girls is a full-nude strip club and adult entertainment venue at 12 N 5th St in downtown Minneapolis — an 18+ gentlemen's club and late-night cabaret where the stage never stops. Because we're a juice bar and serve no alcohol, our doors are open to guests 18 and older. See cover and nightly specials before you head down. We draw guests from across the Twin Cities and a 30-mile radius of downtown Minneapolis.

Every guest orders one beverage on the way in — sodas, juices, energy drinks and mocktails — and the popcorn's always hot and always free. Come for a set, stay for the show — meet the dancers — and on weekends we run right up to 4AM.
