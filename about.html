About Dream Girls, a full-nude strip club in Minneapolis

An 18+ full-nude cabaret and juice bar, built for the night.

Dream Girls is a full-nude strip club and adult entertainment venue at 12 N 5th St in downtown Minneapolis — an 18+ gentlemen's club and late-night cabaret where the stage never stops. Because we're a juice bar and serve no alcohol, our doors are open to guests 18 and older. See cover and nightly specials before you head down.

Every guest orders one beverage on the way in — sodas, juices, energy drinks and mocktails — and the popcorn's always hot and always free. Come for a set, stay for the show — meet the dancers — and on weekends we run right up to 4AM.

  • 18+ full-nude gentlemen's club & juice bar
  • No alcohol — every guest orders one beverage
  • Free popcorn every night we're open
  • Open till 4AM Friday & Saturday
