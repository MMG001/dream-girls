Strip club cover & specials in Minneapolis

Know the full damage before you leave the house. Here's how the door works at downtown Minneapolis' full-nude strip club, every night we're open.

1

$10 cover till midnight / $15 after midnight

Standard cover after midnight gets you into the room — every stage, all night.

2

$14 first drink

We're a juice bar — every guest orders one beverage (sodas, juices, energy drinks, mocktails). After midnight that's $14; plan on $29 to walk in with a drink in hand, and you'll know the full damage before you leave the house.

3

Dances from $20

Topless and nude lap dances start at $20 on the floor. Private dances and the VIP room are priced at the club — ask your entertainer or the floor host.

4

Free popcorn

Fresh, hot popcorn is free every single night the club is open — grab a box, catch the show, come back for refills.

5

Free admission with event ticket stubs

Concerts or sporting events — always free admission with a same-day event ticket stub. Catch the game, concert, or show downtown, then bring your stub in that night and the cover's on us.

6

Free cover birthday week

It's your birthday week — bring a valid ID and your cover is free. Celebrating with a crew? See birthday and group nights.

7

Free cover for bachelor & bachelorette groups

Rolling six or more? Bachelor and bachelorette parties get in free.

8

Free cover from Sneaky Pete's

Coming from our sister club? Sneaky Pete's has the full bar and food — walk over and your cover here is free.

9

Ages: 18+ at Dream Girls, 21+ at Sneaky Pete's

Dream Girls is 18 and up. Our sister club Sneaky Pete's, with its full bar, is 21 and up.
