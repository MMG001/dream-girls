Privacy policy

Last updated: September 2026

Dream Girls ("we") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect through this website and how we use it.

What we collect

This site has no accounts and no tracking cookies of its own. When you use one of our forms — employment, showgirl audition or party booking — the details you enter are sent to us by email through your own email app. We receive only what you choose to send: typically your name, phone, email and message.

How we use it

To respond to your application, audition request or booking. We do not sell, rent or share your information with third parties for marketing.

Age verification

This site asks you to confirm you are 18 or older before entering. That confirmation is stored only in your own browser so you aren't asked again; it is not sent to us.

Third-party services

Pages may load fonts from Google Fonts and link to Google Maps and our social accounts on X, Instagram and Facebook. Those services have their own privacy policies.

In-club privacy

To protect our entertainers and guests, photography and recording are prohibited inside the club. See our terms & conditions.
